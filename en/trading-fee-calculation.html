Fees are the cost of trading that nobody notices but everybody pays. Let's work out how they're calculated, then how to cut them.

The rates below are Binance's standard rates for regular users as shown on its fee page. Always check the official page for current numbers.

Spot fees

Regular user: 0.1% maker, 0.1% taker.
Pay fees with BNB for 25% off: 0.075%.

Formula: fee = trade amount × rate.

Example: buy $1,000 of crypto, pay 1,000 × 0.1% = $1. You pay again when you sell.

USDⓈ-M futures fees

Regular user (USDT-margined): 0.02% maker, 0.05% taker.
Pay with BNB for 10% off: 0.018% maker, 0.045% taker.

Two things to know about futures:

  • Fees are based on position size, not margin. $1,000 at 20x is a $20,000 position.
  • You pay when you open and again when you close.

Example: $1,000, 20x, market order to open and market order to close:
Open: 20,000 × 0.05% = $10
Close: 20,000 × 0.05% = $10
That's $20 for one round trip, or 2% of your $1,000 margin. For active futures traders, fees are the biggest cost.

Maker vs. taker

Maker: you place an order at a price and wait for someone to fill it, like a limit order that doesn't fill immediately.
Taker: you fill immediately at market price, like a market order.

Maker fees are lower, so don't chase with market orders when you don't have to.
