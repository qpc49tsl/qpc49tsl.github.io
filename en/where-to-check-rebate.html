People ask me this all the time: I used a referral code, so where's my rebate? How do I check it?

Short answer: there's no separate "rebate page" for you. Your rebate is just USDC landing in your Spot wallet, a little at a time. So that's where you look.

How the rebate is paid

According to Binance's referral FAQ, since February 2025 it works like this:

  • calculated every hour;
  • converted to USDC;
  • paid into your Spot wallet within 6 hours.

Trade in the morning, and you'll see it by the afternoon at the latest. Nothing to claim, nothing to click.

Checking in the app

  1. Open the Binance app and tap Assets at the bottom.
  2. Go to Spot.
  3. Find USDC and tap it to see its history.

Small USDC credits coming in roughly every hour are your rebates. If you paid 5 USDT in fees in an hour, you'll get about 1 USDC back.

Checking on the website

Log in on binance.com, go to Wallet → Spot, and look at the transaction history for USDC. You can export the history and add it up in a spreadsheet to see your total for the month.

Traded but no rebate? Usually one of these

  • It hasn't been paid yet. It can take up to 6 hours.
  • The trade had no fee. Zero-fee pairs and zero-maker-fee futures promotions don't charge a fee, so there's nothing to rebate. Rebates are based on fees you actually pay.
  • You're VIP 3 or higher. Spot rebates stop at VIP 3.
  • Your region isn't eligible. Residents of the UK, the EU/EEA, Singapore, Japan, Israel, Turkey and Hong Kong can't take part in Binance's referral program.
  • The code was never bound to your account. You skipped it at sign-up, or a later binding didn't go through. See below.
