How the split works

Every time you pay a trading fee, Binance gives part of it to your referrer. That's the commission. The referrer can share part of it with you. That's your rebate, or kickback.

Binance's own FAQ says the invited user's kickback is capped at 20%. On futures, Binance also limits how much a referrer can share with friends to 20%.

So where do 40% and 50% come from?

They're referrer tiers. Binance reviews referrers every quarter based on how many new traders they bring in and how much those traders trade:

Spot: 20% by default, then 30%, 41% and 50%.
Futures: 10% by default, then 30%, 40% and 50%.

A Super Referral member at 50% can still only share up to 20% with you. The rest is theirs. "50% rebate" isn't a lie, it's just not your 50%.

Watch out for these "high rebate" offers

  • Anyone offering you 30% or 40% back is either describing their own rate as yours, or paying you privately outside Binance's system, with zero guarantees.
  • Anyone asking for your API keys or login "to handle your rebate": walk away.
  • Anyone asking you to send money to an address first: that's a scam.

A real referral rebate only needs a code at sign-up. Nothing else.
