1. What a referral rebate actually is

When you pay a trading fee, Binance gives part of it to whoever referred you. The referrer can pass part of that on to you. The part you get is called a "kickback" or rebate.

With QPC49TSL, you get 20% of your trading fees back, on both spot and futures.

A few details:

  • It's calculated every hour, converted to USDC, and paid into your Spot wallet within 6 hours (Binance's rule since February 2025).
  • Nothing to claim or apply for. It's automatic.
  • No trading, no rebate. It's based purely on the fees you actually pay.

5. Already bound to someone else's code?

You can't change it. Binance's FAQ is clear: once a referral relationship is set, it can't be changed or removed.

Closing your account and opening a new one with the same ID won't work either. Binance detects it, the rebates on the new account get terminated, and you end up with nothing.

6. How long the rebate lasts

Most posts skip this part.

Spot: as long as you're between VIP 0 and VIP 2. Once you hit VIP 3, it stops. At that level your base fees are already much lower anyway.

Futures: this is where normal codes and Super Referral codes differ. With a normal code, futures rebates only last 12 months from the day you open your futures account. That limit only goes away if the referrer's futures commission rate is 30% or higher, which is the Super Referral level. QPC49TSL is a Super Referral code, so there's no 12-month cap on futures either. If someone promises "lifetime futures rebates", ask them whether they're a Super Referral member.

7. What about the "30%, 40%, 50%" codes?

Those numbers are the referrer's own commission tiers. Binance reviews referrers every quarter based on how many new traders they bring in and how much their invitees trade. The top tier is 50% for both spot and futures. That's what the referrer earns, not what you get. From what I've seen, regular codes give the invited user 20% at most.
